Tajkart Student Hub is built for students who want to understand a subject, practise a useful skill, build something they can explain, and plan a next step. Self-learners and early-career learners are welcome too. Start with a goal and follow an original route through lessons, practice, projects, and career guidance.

The library is growing in reviewed batches. Check the [content progress report]({{ '/docs/CONTENT_PROGRESS/' | relative_url }}) for what is drafted, what has been validated automatically, and what still needs human review.
