There are no currently listed or verified offers on this site. This page explains what we would check before publishing one; it is not an offer directory.

## What a listing must say

A future listing should identify the provider, official application or offer page, region, eligibility, important terms, expiry date if given, and the date Tajkart checked it. If a detail cannot be confirmed, the listing should say so plainly.

## How to check an opportunity

Start from the provider's official website rather than an unsolicited message. Confirm eligibility and deadlines with the provider. Be cautious with requests for fees, passwords, banking details, or urgent action. A listed opportunity does not mean Tajkart guarantees its validity, admission, hiring, or a discount.

Expired or changed entries should be removed or clearly marked, not presented as current. See the [offer template]({{ '/templates/OFFER_TEMPLATE/' | relative_url }}) and [content standards]({{ '/docs/CONTENT_STANDARDS/' | relative_url }}).
