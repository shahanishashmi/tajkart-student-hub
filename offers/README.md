# Tajkart Student Offers

This section is intended for legitimate student discounts and learning offers. Offers are time-sensitive and must be verified before publication.

## Required listing fields
- Provider and offer title
- What is included and what is excluded
- Eligibility and geographic restrictions
- Price, renewal terms, and cancellation conditions when applicable
- Official offer or application page
- Verification date and known expiry date
- Any Tajkart affiliate, sponsorship, or commercial relationship

## Trust rules
Never invent a discount, imply a partnership that does not exist, hide recurring charges, or guarantee eligibility. Do not ask students to share passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, or unnecessary sensitive information. Mark expired offers promptly.

No specific third-party offer is advertised in this starter repository; listings will be added only after verification.
