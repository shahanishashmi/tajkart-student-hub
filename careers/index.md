Career preparation is a process of gathering evidence about your skills and communicating it clearly. This hub provides general guidance; it does not promise interviews, jobs, or placement outcomes. No jobs are currently listed here.

## Build evidence

Choose a small project that solves a real problem for you. Keep a short README with its purpose, setup steps, example use, and known limits. Use Git to track progress; see the [Git commits lesson]({{ '/learning/lessons/git-small-commits/' | relative_url }}).

## Explain your work

For each project, be ready to describe the problem, your choices, a challenge you met, how you checked the result, and what you would improve. Be accurate about your role, the tools you used, and what is finished.

Use the [resume and portfolio guide]({{ '/careers/resume-portfolio/' | relative_url }}) to present that evidence, then practise with the [interview preparation guide]({{ '/careers/interview-prep/' | relative_url }}).

## Prepare a résumé

- Put relevant skills and project evidence where a reader can find them quickly.
- Describe actions and outcomes you can support with real details.
- Use a clean, readable format and check spelling and links.
- Tailor the order and wording to the role while keeping every claim truthful.
- Remove personal information that an application does not need.

## Verify opportunities

Confirm the employer through its official site, understand the role and application terms, and do not pay a recruiter to guarantee a job. Check suspicious requests with a trusted adult, career adviser, or relevant institution before sharing sensitive information. Use the [student opportunity checklist]({{ '/offers/' | relative_url }}) to review scholarships, internships, competitions, or offers; this site does not currently list active opportunities.
