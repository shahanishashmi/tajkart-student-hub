# Tajkart Career Hub

Career guidance here is original educational content. Individual job, internship, or scholarship listings should be verified separately and must not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.

The [Career Hub](index.md) links five guides covering resumes, interviews, internship preparation, professional email, and entry-level learning paths. It does not list live jobs or promise placements.

## Career preparation tracks

### Explore
Identify interests, current skills, constraints, and preferred work style. Compare roles by daily responsibilities rather than title alone.

### Build evidence
Create two or three small projects with clear READMEs, setup instructions, screenshots where useful, tests, and honest explanations of limitations.

### Present your work
Write a concise resume focused on evidence and impact. Keep claims truthful, use links that work, and remove personal information that is not needed for an application.

### Prepare
Practise explaining projects, debugging unfamiliar problems, fundamental concepts, and behavioural examples. Review the employer and role before an interview.

### Apply safely
Use official employer channels where possible. Be cautious of requests for fees, passwords, OTPs, bank credentials, or sensitive identity documents before legitimacy is established.
