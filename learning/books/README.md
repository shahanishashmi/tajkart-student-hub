# Books and Reading Guides

Tajkart's reading guides help learners use technical books effectively. This section does not redistribute books or reproduce third-party book descriptions.

## How to choose a technical book

1. Match the book to your current level and goal.
2. Check the edition, publication date, and whether examples match the tools you use.
3. Preview the table of contents and one sample chapter.
4. Prefer resources with exercises, explanations, and clear prerequisites.
5. Confirm that a free copy is legally offered before downloading it.

## Active reading method

- Preview the chapter and write down two questions.
- Read a small section, then explain it without looking.
- Recreate each example in your own environment.
- Change one input or assumption and predict the result.
- Record mistakes and revisit them after a day and a week.
