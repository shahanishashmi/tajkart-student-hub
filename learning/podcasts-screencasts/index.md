Audio and video can introduce an idea, but passive watching makes it hard to tell what you can apply. Use a short active routine:

1. Pick one question before pressing play.
2. Pause when a new term or code example appears; write a short prediction.
3. Recreate the example without copying it line by line.
4. Summarise the main point from memory and list anything still unclear.
5. Practise the skill on a small problem of your own.

Check whether the creator explains prerequisites, dates, and tool versions. Prefer captions or transcripts when available, and adjust playback or take breaks to suit your needs. Tajkart does not endorse a channel simply by describing this learning method.
