This is a short English draft that follows arithmetic, fractions, decimals, ratios, and percentages. It introduces expressions and equations; it is not a complete algebra curriculum, accredited course, grade guarantee, or substitute for a teacher's sequence.

## Who should start here

Begin after the [Mathematics Foundations arithmetic path]({{ '/learning/courses/academic-foundations/' | relative_url }}). It ends with percentages and proportion, then this path moves to variables and equations. If fractions or negative numbers are unfamiliar, review the earlier lessons first.

The estimate is a planning aid only; learners may take more or less time. No streak or daily deadline is required. The browser-local [study planner]({{ '/learning/planner/' | relative_url }}) can break authored paths into sessions without an account or synchronization.

## How to work

For each question, write the variable meaning, operation, and reason for each step. Open a hint only after an attempt. For an equation, substitute the answer into the original statement. A correct numeric result with an unbalanced transformation still needs revision.

## Completion and limits

You can explain a variable expression, evaluate it, solve the introductory equation forms, model a short fixed-plus-rate situation, and check a result in context. These skills are a starting point; inequalities, graphs, systems, polynomials, and broader algebra remain outside this short sequence. All lessons and the self-check are pending human mathematics review.
