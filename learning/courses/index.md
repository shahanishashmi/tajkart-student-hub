This plan is a suggested sequence, not a credential or an accredited course. Adapt the pace to your available time. Each session can be 25 to 45 minutes; consistency matters more than finishing on a fixed date.

## Before you begin

Choose one language and set up a way to run short programs. Keep a learning log with three prompts: What did I try? What surprised me? What will I try next?

## Week 1 — Values and decisions

Learn names, common value types, input/output, and conditional choices. Complete the [variables lesson]({{ '/learning/lessons/python-variables/' | relative_url }}). Build a tiny program that calculates a total or converts a unit.

## Week 2 — Repetition and decomposition

Practise loops and functions. Complete the [loop lesson]({{ '/learning/lessons/problem-solving-loops/' | relative_url }}). Build a program that processes several scores or study sessions.

## Week 3 — Collections and problem solving

Use lists and dictionaries to organise related values. Solve a handful of small problems: count items, find a maximum, and summarise a set of scores. Explain each solution before coding it.

## Week 4 — Build and review

Choose a personal project with a clear input and output, such as a reading tracker or flashcard quiz. Save progress with [small Git commits]({{ '/learning/lessons/git-small-commits/' | relative_url }}). Test an empty input, a typical input, and an unusual input.

## Check your progress

You are ready to move on when you can explain your code, change it without losing track of its behavior, and find at least one mistake by testing. Revisit the [subject map]({{ '/learning/subjects/' | relative_url }}) to choose your next topic.
