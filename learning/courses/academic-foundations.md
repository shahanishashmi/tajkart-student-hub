This original self-study path connects five short mathematics lessons and one mixed practice set, then points to introductory algebra. It is a first sequence, not a complete school mathematics curriculum, a grade guarantee, or a replacement for your class syllabus. Plan roughly three and a half hours for reading and first attempts; your pace may differ.

## Who this path is for

Start here if you can multiply and divide whole numbers and want to refresh fraction operations and everyday number conversions. If those operations are not comfortable yet, use your teacher's materials or practise them first; every worked answer below shows a check you can follow.

## How to study

For each lesson, write one prediction before reading the answer, work at least two practice questions, and compare your method rather than only the final number. Keep exact fractions through intermediate steps. Round only when the question asks for it; currency examples state a rounding rule explicitly.

After the mixed set, mark which step needs another example and revisit that lesson. The score is a private formative check, not an official grade. A teacher's required notation, rounding convention, and assessment rules take precedence.

## Completion check and next steps

You can show equivalent steps for a fraction operation, convert a common value among fraction/decimal/percent forms, solve a unit-rate question with units, explain a percent baseline, and compare a small data set with a stated summary. Continue into the separate [algebra foundations path]({{ '/learning/courses/algebra-foundations/' | relative_url }}) when those ideas are comfortable. For another area of study, return to the [student start guide]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}) or the [subject map]({{ '/learning/subjects/' | relative_url }}). The current mathematics coverage remains short and English-only; additional units should follow learner demand and educator review.
