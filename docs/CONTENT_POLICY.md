Tajkart Student Hub publishes original learning material for students and independent learners. Educational usefulness, accuracy, accessibility, and clear rights are more important than the number of pages.

## Publication rules

- Write explanations, exercises, examples, translations, and project briefs for Tajkart. Do not copy curated entries or prose from a reference archive.
- Link to an external work only when the link is relevant and lawful. A directory entry does not mean Tajkart owns or endorses the linked work.
- Do not upload copyrighted books, paid course materials, answer keys, logos, screenshots, or course videos without permission and a recorded licence.
- Cite primary documentation for version-sensitive technical details. Record a last-checked date for live opportunities and changing external resources.
- Never invent a provider, credential, offer, deadline, job, salary, partnership, testimonial, or learning outcome.
- Avoid guarantees. Study times and course plans are estimates, not accreditation or placement promises.
- Do not collect student data or run submitted source code on a server. Any future interactive execution needs an independently reviewed isolation design.

## Corrections and removals

Open an issue or pull request with the page route, the exact problem, and a reliable source or reproduction. For a rights or privacy concern, contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile. Do not include personal student records in public issues. Correct or remove unsafe, inaccurate, expired, or infringing material promptly and update the progress inventory.
