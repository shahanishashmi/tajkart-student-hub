Tajkart Student Hub welcomes learners and contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and access needs. Participation in project spaces is conditional on treating others with care.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive when discussing work or asking questions.
- Critique ideas and examples without attacking a person's identity, ability, or intent.
- Make space for beginner questions and acknowledge when a claim needs evidence.
- Protect privacy. Do not share another person's contact details, student records, or private messages without consent.
- Accept correction, explain decisions, and update material when a credible issue is found.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, targeted insults, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, and knowingly sharing private information are not acceptable. Do not use project discussions to advertise scams, pirated material, or unsafe opportunities.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication. Report a concern privately to the repository owner using the contact method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish personal details in an issue. If no private contact method is available, open a brief issue asking for a private reporting route without describing the incident publicly.

The project owner will review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and decide on proportionate action. A report is not a public verdict; people involved should avoid retaliation or speculation while it is being reviewed.
