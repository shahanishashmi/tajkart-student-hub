Tajkart Student Hub is a learning and opportunity project for students, self-learners, and early-career professionals. It publishes original lessons, practice, and study guidance.

## Founder & Educator

**Shah Anis Hashmi** is the Founder & Educator of Tajkart Student Hub. This page intentionally makes no claims about qualifications or biography beyond that role.

## Our approach

Learning material should make its goals clear, show examples, invite practice, and identify where advice may change over time. The repository is developed in the open so readers can review how content is organised and propose corrections.

## Contact and contributions

Use the repository's contribution process to suggest a correction or an original lesson. Read the [content standards]({{ '/docs/CONTENT_STANDARDS/' | relative_url }}) before submitting educational material.
